# KABIDAK National Liver Care Intelligence & Operations Platform — Operational MVP

Public operational demonstration using simulated data only.

## Demonstrated flows
- Executive operational command center
- Beneficiary journey continuity
- Closed-loop referrals
- Tarhal readiness and stop impact
- Medication-access continuity
- Donation-to-impact and funding sustainability
- Volunteer/workforce coordination
- Partner service-level monitoring
- Operational scenario simulation
- Local audit trail
- Scope map for 125 platform features

## Safety boundary
This public MVP does not diagnose, prescribe, determine transplant eligibility, donor eligibility, organ matching/allocation, or replace authorized clinical decisions.

All names, cases, amounts and operational events in the demo are simulated.
